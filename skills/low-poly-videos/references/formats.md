# Formats

Choose the format the user named, or the one the idea suits best. Aspect ratios available: `9:16` (default), `16:9`, `1:1`, `4:5`. In wide formats keep the same small-subject framing, with captions still in the upper third.

## Choose (a choice between worlds)

Trigger: "choose a...", "pick a...", "which would you...", "would you rather", any set of options the viewer imagines living in.

Length: 90 to 135 s for four options, 40 to 70 s for two.

Structure:
1. Hook shot showing all the options numbered, with a big title such as "Pick a Door" and a line stating the rule.
2. For each option: a title card shot ("Door 1", 1.5 to 2 s), then 5 to 7 shots, around 20 to 30 s per option.
3. Each option starts appealing, then turns. Reveal the catch late and specifically. The final option can be the worst or strangely the best.
4. A closing shot returns to the options and asks which one the viewer chooses.

Style notes: calm second-person narration, male voice at rate -1, `caption_style: "phrase"`, recurring small avatar with a backpack, music section changes at each option and at each turn.

Options should differ visually at a glance: change the light preset, ground colour and silhouette.

## How to build (cheerful explainer)

Trigger: "how to build...", "how X is made", "how does X work".

Length: 60 to 95 s.

Structure:
1. Hook with the finished thing, or a big problem.
2. Numbered stages. Each stage gets 2 to 4 very short shots.
3. Include at least one cutaway of layers or inner workings, built from stacked boxes with labelled signs.
4. Comedic escalation: a giant prop, weather interrupting, a worker reacting.
5. End on the finished result, used.

Style notes: chirpy female voice (`af_bella` or `af_heart`) at rate +1, `caption_style: "word"`, one word on screen at a time with one `*keyword*` per phrase highlighted, `word_y` alternating `high` and `low`, shots around 1.5 to 2.5 s, chibi character (`chibi: true`, `hat: 'hardhat'`, `vest: '#d6ff3f'`), playful music, frequent `pop`, `boing`, `thud` and `whoosh`.

## Story (a tiny narrative)

Trigger: "a story about...", "what if...", or any idea with events and no options.

Length: 40 to 120 s.

Structure: set the ordinary world in two shots, disturb it, escalate three times with a location change each time, then end on a single quiet image or a twist. Past or second person, whichever makes the twist hit harder.

Style notes: calm voice, `caption_style: "phrase"`, music moves from `calm` through `uneasy` to `dread`, or to `heroic` for a triumphant ending.

## Explainer (facts with images)

Trigger: "what happens if...", "why does...", "the history of...", "top 5...".

Length: 45 to 120 s.

Structure: open with the most surprising fact. Give one fact per shot and use a visual comparison for scale, such as a tiny person beside a huge object. Numbered lists get a title card per item. End on a last fact that reframes the first.

Style notes: neutral voice at rate 0, `caption_style: "phrase"`, `mystery` or `wonder` music. Only state facts that are actually true, and prefer widely established ones.

## Anything else

Fit the request to the nearest format and keep the fundamentals: audio-first timing, vertical small-subject framing, hard cuts and a turn before the ending. If the user specifies a length, voice or mood, that overrides this table.
