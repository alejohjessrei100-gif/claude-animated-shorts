## Facts (checked 2026-11-07)
- Robert Matthews (then at Aston University), "Tumbling toast, Murphy's Law and the fundamental constants",
  European Journal of Physics 16(4):172-176, 1995. Ig Nobel Prize in Physics, 1996. [Wikipedia; oikofuge.com summary]
- Toast sliding off a table edge picks up spin and only completes about half a turn before hitting the floor from normal
  table height, so it lands butter-side down; a table about 3 m high would allow a full turn. He tied the height of
  tables (set by human height) to limits from fundamental constants. [Wikipedia "Buttered toast phenomenon"]
- Toast pushed off at high speed does not rotate enough to land butter-down (the "knock it off fast" fix). [Wikipedia]
- 2001 UK schools experiment (Matthews, Daily Telegraph write-up): almost 10,000 drops, 62 % butter-side down; slices marked
  with a letter "B" instead of butter landed B-side down 58 % of the time (so it is not the butter's weight); from above
  8 ft only 47 % landed face-down. [NPR 9 Mar 2001; foodreference.com; Wikipedia]
