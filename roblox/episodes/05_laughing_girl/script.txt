In this town, every time you laugh, flowers grow where you're standing. Giggle once and you get a daisy. Laugh for a minute and you're knee deep in tulips. And nobody laughed more than Dot. Her front yard was a jungle. Sunflowers blocked the sidewalk. Vines climbed over the neighbors' cars. The mayor gave her a ticket every week and kids called her Weed. So Dot stopped laughing. Then one summer, the rain never came. Every farm in town dried up and turned to dust. The stores ran out of food. Dot walked into the middle of the dead fields with her best friend, who told her the worst jokes he knew. She laughed until sunset. By morning, the whole valley was green. Now everyone in town tells Dot jokes. The bad ones work best.
