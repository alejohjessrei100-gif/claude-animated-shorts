In this town, every time you do something kind, your shoes get heavier. Help someone with their homework and your sneakers gain a pound. Do it every day and you can barely lift your feet. And nobody had heavier shoes than Theo. He stomped down the hallway like a robot. He came last in every race. Kids called him Bricks and laughed every time he tripped. Then one afternoon, a giant windstorm hit the playground. Kids started sliding across the grass. Then their feet left the ground. Teachers grabbed the fence and got blown off it. But Theo didn't move an inch. He grabbed one kid, then another, until the whole class was holding onto him. Turns out his kindness was never weighing him down. It was keeping everyone else on the ground.
