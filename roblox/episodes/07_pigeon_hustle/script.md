# 07 · The Pigeon Hustle 🐦

**Format:** 9:16, 1080×1920, 60 fps, 48.8 s. There are 44 shots, each pinned to a word of the user's Adam recording (×1.5, +6 semitones).

**Variety brief (from the user):** different length, different structure, no bullying, no rescue.

**Structure:** a comedy where a greedy kid's hustle grows and then backfires on him.
1. The rule
2. The narrator spots the business
3. Slow start
4. A rival
5. He makes people complain on purpose
6. The heat wave sends it out of control
7. He gets rich (running gag: bikes, then a spiked helmet)
8. The kindness award
9. He complains about the medal, and the pigeons turn on him

**Rule made visible:** whoever complains gets a pigeon that flies in and lands on their head or shoulders, and it flies off when they say something nice. By Friday, the pigeons cover the rooftops, lamps, the statue and everyone in town.

## Pigeons

- Each pigeon is built from 11 instanced parts: body, iridescent neck, head, beak, orange eyes, tail, pink feet and two wings.
- Up to 700 pigeons are on screen at once, pecking on the ground and flapping when they fly.
- Pigeons that land fly in on an arc, ride the person's head and peck now and then.
- On "turned around", every pigeon in town turns to face the narrator.
