In my town, when you get nervous, everyone can hear your heartbeat. A little nervous, and the kid next to you hears it. Really scared, and the whole room does. And nobody's heart was louder than mine. It boomed every time a teacher called my name. Kids called me Drum, and slammed lockers just to make it pound. Then, on our class ferry trip, a fog rolled in so thick we couldn't see the front of the boat. The lighthouse went dark. The captain couldn't see the rocks. Everyone went quiet. Except me. My heart was pounding so loud, it bounced off the cliffs. The captain heard the echo, and steered toward the silence, all the way to the harbor. Turns out my heart was never too loud. It was the only one loud enough to bring us home.
