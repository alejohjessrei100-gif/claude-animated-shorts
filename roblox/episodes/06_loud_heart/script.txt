In this town, when you get nervous, everyone can hear your heartbeat. A little nervous and the kid next to you hears it. Really scared and the whole room does. And nobody had a louder heart than Rudy. It boomed every time a teacher called his name. Kids called him Drum and slammed lockers just to make it pound. Then on the class ferry trip, a fog rolled in so thick you couldn't see the front of the boat. The lighthouse went dark. The captain couldn't see the rocks. Rudy was terrified. His heart thundered across the water, and the echo bounced back off the cliffs. The captain steered toward the silence, all the way to the harbor. Turns out Rudy's heart was never too loud. It was the only one loud enough to bring them home.
