In this town, whenever you get scared, you glow. A little nervous and your fingers shine. Really terrified and you light up like a lamp. And nobody glowed brighter than Ollie. He was scared of dogs, thunder, and talking in class, so he lit up every single day. Kids called him Nightlight and threw blankets over him at recess. Then one night, the power went out across the whole town during the school play. The halls went pitch black. Kids screamed. Teachers tripped over chairs. Nobody could find the exits. Ollie was more terrified than anyone. So he shined brighter than ever. He took the closest kid's hand and walked the whole school out, step by step, glowing the entire way. They still call him Nightlight. But now it's because he's the one they follow home.
